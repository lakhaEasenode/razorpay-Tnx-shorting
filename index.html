Razorpay Transaction Processor

About this tool

Turns Razorpay's Combined Report into journal-ready CSV files for Zoho Books. Each payment is split into two entries: the payment (gross amount, credit) and the Razorpay fee (debit). Settlements and refunds are kept as single entries. Pick a date range and download.

Your file is processed in this browser only. It is never uploaded to any server.

How to get the CSV from Razorpay
  1. Log in to your Razorpay Dashboard.
  2. Open Reports: dashboard.razorpay.com/app/reports
  3. Under Transactions, select Combined Report.
  4. Click Download Report and choose the duration you need.
  5. Go to the Downloads section and download the latest report.
  6. If it downloads as a .zip, unzip it first to get the .csv file.
  7. Upload the CSV below and click Process file.
